# K151 audio driver compile-only integration

Status: **NOT APPROVED FOR DEVICE INSTALLATION**. No hardware touched.

Pinned upstream source evidence:
- [M5Stack StackChan board pins](https://github.com/m5stack/StackChan/blob/28b9f0c8bd6acd81cb3c70f1a93c3e373b39d400/firmware/main/hal/board/config.h): 24kHz; GPIO0/33/34/14/13 I2S; GPIO12/11 I2C.
- [M5Stack StackChan board construction](https://github.com/m5stack/StackChan/blob/28b9f0c8bd6acd81cb3c70f1a93c3e373b39d400/firmware/main/hal/board/stackchan.cc): AW9523 (0x58) resets AW88298; AXP2101 (0x34) sets rails; power/LCD shared.
- [xiaozhi-esp32 pin-compatible codec](https://github.com/78/xiaozhi-esp32/blob/49b4380b3faa0ceadd28a3e78aaf0f311c46c57e/main/boards/m5stack/core-s3/cores3_audio_codec.cc): ESP-IDF STD TX and four-slot TDM RX. The upstream Read/Write returns requested sample counts even if esp_codec_dev_read/write errors; the new adapter treats nonzero errors as faults.
- [Espressif esp_codec_dev 1.6.2](https://components.espressif.com/components/espressif/esp_codec_dev/versions/1.6.2): exact 1.x dependency, ES7210/AW88298.

New `main/k151_codec_port.*` compiles the full digital audio bus + codec interface and explicitly gates `prepare()` on three independent booleans. No caller currently supplies these. `app_main` references only a compiled-link marker and remains HOLD forever. No I2S/I2C channel or codec instance is created by `app_main`. Input can only be opened while output is closed; both capture and playback are limited to 10ms chunks. Every esp_codec_dev read/write result is checked and a failure latches the fault. No raw audio storage/transmission/logging. This is not evidence of live microphone capture or final audio drain.

**NOT DONE**: actual AW9523 reset and AXP2101 rails; initial amplifier mute; LCD C/E identification; validating K151 board revision; real I2S reference-channel mapping; interruptible STOP latency for synchronous codec calls; DMA/heap/PSRAM measurements; physical sample rates and timbre; integration into AudioGuard, VAD/wake/GPT pipeline. Hardware start remains deliberately inaccessible from the compile-only app.

The test/build result may prove compilation and linking of `k151_codec_port.cpp`, NOT that the audio hardware operates. Do not enable hardware, flash, OTA, overwrite NVS or merge into main. Maintain factory restoration HOLD.
